      About PresLink

      Students in Cork, schools in Ghana and Nigeria.

      PresLink is the student-run charity of Presentation Brothers College, Cork. It was started in 2005 by a group of junior students, and every year a new committee of students takes it on, with teachers as mentors.

      It raises money for the schools and missions run by the Presentation Brothers in Ghana and Nigeria. The Brothers are a Catholic congregation founded by Blessed Edmund Rice in Waterford in 1802, and they work in Ireland, Ghana, Nigeria and the West Indies.

      What PresLink has funded

      🏫
      Classrooms in Bolgatanga

      A three-classroom block at the new Presentation Primary School in Sokabisi, Bolgatanga, Ghana. In 2021 the school had 107 pupils and four staff.

      🚲
      Bikes to school

      A 2018 campaign to provide bicycles so that girls in Ghana and Nigeria can travel to school and stay in education.

      🍼
      Mother of Mercy Babies Home

      Donations to the babies home in Sirigu, Ghana, which the Brothers' community in Bolgatanga supports.

      🎄
      Year after year

      Table quizzes, bag-packs, carol concerts, fantasy football and a Christmas raffle. In 2017 the students sent €6,160 to the Brothers' West Africa Province.

      Where your €2 goes

      Every play is a payment to PresLink. Stripe's card fee, about 1.5% plus 25 cent, is the only deduction. The PresLink committee passes the money on to the Presentation Brothers' mission office for their schools in Ghana and Nigeria.

      Facts from the Pres Gazette newsletters (2016 to 2021) and presentationbrothers.org. The committee keeps this page up to date.

        Terms & conditions

        Last updated 22 October 2026

        1. Who runs this

        PresLink Arcade is run by the PresLink student committee of Presentation Brothers College, Cork (“the Committee”), with staff mentors. Questions: [committee contact email].

        2. What it is

        A game of skill. Each play costs €2, or €5 for 3 plays and €10 for 7. All payments, less Stripe's card-processing fees, go to PresLink for the Presentation Brothers' schools and projects in Ghana and Nigeria.

        3. Who can play

        Students and staff of Presentation Brothers College and their supporters. Under 18s must have a parent's or guardian's permission before paying. The Committee may refuse or remove any player.

        4. Your name and claim code

        Your phone is given a random three-letter name the first time you play. You can change it on the You tab; each name can be used by one person only and offensive names are blocked or removed. Your four-digit claim code proves a score is yours and signs you in on another phone. Keep it private.

        5. How winning works

        A competition week runs Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59, Irish time. The highest score of the week on the Students board and on the Teachers board wins. Equal scores: the earlier one wins. Winners are announced by the Committee [where and when] and must show their letters and claim code to collect. The prize is set by the Committee each week and has no cash alternative.

        6. Fair play

        One tap per play. Scores are checked by our server. Plays may be voided and players removed for cheating or tampering. The Committee's decision is final.

        7. Payments and refunds

        Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see your card number. Plays are not refundable except where the law requires it or a fault on our side meant a paid play could not be used. Contact the Committee and we will put it right.

        8. Your data

        We store your three letters, whether you play on the student or teacher board, your scores and play times, and a record of each payment (amount, time, Stripe reference and the email you give Stripe for your receipt). We do not ask for your name. Data is held by Supabase (UK/EU) and Stripe and is deleted when the campaign ends. To have it removed sooner, contact the Committee with your letters and claim code.

        9. Availability

        The game may be unavailable at times. We are not responsible for plays lost because of your device, network or browser, though we will try to help.

        10. Changes and law

        The Committee may change these terms, prices or prizes, or end the game, at any time. These terms are governed by the laws of Ireland.
