What is PresLink?

PresLink is the student-run charity of Presentation Brothers College in Cork. A new committee of students takes it on every year, with teachers as mentors, and raises money for the schools and missions the Presentation Brothers run in Ghana and Nigeria.

  • 2005Started by a group of junior students at Pres
  • Ghana & NigeriaWhere the Brothers run the schools you're funding
  • 100%Of what you pay, after card fees, goes to PresLink

What your €2 does

🏫

Classrooms

PresLink financed a three-classroom block at Presentation Primary School in Sokabisi, Bolgatanga, Ghana. It opened with 107 pupils.

🚲

Bikes to school

Bicycles so girls in Ghana and Nigeria can make the journey to school and stay in education.

🍼

Babies home

Support for the Mother of Mercy Babies Home in Sirigu, Ghana, which the Brothers' community in Bolgatanga looks after.

1. Who runs this

PresLink Arcade is run by the PresLink student committee of Presentation Brothers College, Cork (“the Committee”), with staff mentors. Questions: [committee contact email].

2. What it is

PresLink Arcade is a game of skill. Each play costs €2. You can also buy 3 plays for €5 or 7 plays for €10. All payments, less the card-processing fees charged by Stripe, go to PresLink for the Presentation Brothers' schools and projects in Ghana and Nigeria.

3. Who can play

The game is for students and staff of Presentation Brothers College and their supporters. If you are under 18 you must have the permission of a parent or guardian before you pay. The Committee may refuse or remove any player.

4. Your arcade name and claim code

You play under three letters of your choice. Names that are offensive or impersonate someone will be removed. Your device remembers your player. The app also gives you a four-digit claim code. Keep it: it is how you prove a winning score is yours.

5. How winning works

Each competition week runs from Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59, Irish time. The highest score of the week on the Students board and on the Teachers board wins. If two scores are equal, the one achieved first wins. Winners are announced by the Committee [where and when, e.g. at Monday assembly] and must show their arcade name and claim code on their device to collect. The prize is set and announced by the Committee each week and has no cash alternative.

6. Fair play

One tap per play. Scores are checked by our server. Plays may be voided, and players removed, for cheating, tampering with the game, or interfering with other players. The Committee's decision is final.

7. Payments and refunds

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Plays are not refundable, except where the law requires it or where a technical fault on our side meant a paid play could not be used. In that case contact the Committee and we will put it right.

8. Your data

We store your three letters, whether you are a student or teacher, your scores and play times, and a record of each payment (amount, time, Stripe reference and the email address you give Stripe for your receipt). We do not ask for your name. Data is held by Supabase (EU) and Stripe and is deleted when the campaign ends. To have your data removed sooner, contact the Committee with your arcade name and claim code.

9. Availability

We will do our best to keep the game running, but it may be unavailable at times. We are not responsible for plays lost because of your device, network or browser, though we will try to help.

10. Changes

The Committee may change these terms, prices or prizes, or end the game, at any time. Changes are posted on this page.

11. Law

These terms are governed by the laws of Ireland.

By playing you agree to the terms above. Under 18? Ask a parent or guardian before you pay.
