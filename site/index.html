      How to play

      Terms

      Last updated 23 October 2026

      1. Who runs this

      PresLink Arcade is run by the PresLink student committee of Presentation Brothers College, Cork (“the Committee”), with staff mentors. Questions go to the PresLink committee.

      2. What it is

      A game of skill. Each play costs €2, or €5 for 3 plays and €10 for 7. All payments, less Stripe's card-processing fees, go to PresLink for the Presentation Brothers' schools and projects in Ghana and Nigeria.

      3. Who can play

      Anyone. Under 18s must have a parent's or guardian's permission before paying. The Committee may refuse or remove any player.

      4. Your letters, code and boards

      You play under two or three letters of your choice. If someone already has them you are shown with a number, for example JAK#2. Offensive names are blocked or removed. Your four-digit code proves a score is yours and signs you in on another device. Keep it private. Everyone is on the Everyone board. Students and teachers of Presentation Brothers College may also join the Students or Teachers board by giving their school email address, which the Committee may check.

      5. Prizes

      Prizes are awarded once, at the end of the campaign, for the highest score on the Everyone, Students and Teachers boards, and spot prizes for the best single light and the most plays. Equal scores: the earlier one wins. Winners are announced by the Committee at the end of the campaign and collect by showing their letters and code. Prizes are set by the Committee and have no cash alternative.

      6. Fair play

      A play is three lights; the score is the average of the three. Scores are checked by our server. Plays may be voided and players removed for cheating or tampering. The Committee's decision is final.

      7. Payments and refunds

      Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see your card number. Plays are not refundable except where the law requires it or a fault on our side meant a paid play could not be used. Contact the Committee and we will put it right.

      8. Your data

      We store your letters, your board, your school email if you give one, your scores and play times, and a record of each payment (amount, time, Stripe reference and the email you give Stripe for your receipt). Data is held by Supabase (UK/EU) and Stripe and is deleted when the campaign ends. To have it removed sooner, contact the Committee with your letters and code.

      9. Availability

      The game may be unavailable at times. We are not responsible for plays lost because of your device, network or browser, though we will try to help.

      10. About the facts

      Facts about PresLink on this site come from the school's Pres Gazette newsletters and presentationbrothers.org. The Committee keeps them up to date.

      11. Changes and law

      The Committee may change these terms, prices or prizes, or end the game, at any time. These terms are governed by the laws of Ireland.
