        Terms

        Last updated 28 October 2026

        In short. A game of skill. €2 a play, 3 for €5 or 7 for €10. Under 18s need a parent's or guardian's permission to pay. Unused plays can be refunded.

        1. Who runs this

        PresLink Arcade is run by PresLink, the student charity committee of Presentation Brothers College, The Mardyke, Cork T12 VK49 (“we”), with staff mentors. You can contact us through the school.

        2. What you are buying

        A play is three lights, and your score is the average of the three. Plays cost €2 each, €5 for 3 or €10 for 7. They are added to your player straight away and can be used until the game closes. Everything you pay, less Stripe's card fees, goes to PresLink for the Presentation Brothers' schools and projects in Ghana and Nigeria.

        3. Who can play

        Anyone. Under 18s need a parent's or guardian's permission before paying. One player per person. Committee members and staff mentors can play but can't win prizes.

        4. Your initials, code and boards

        You play under two or three initials of your choice. If someone already has them you are shown with a number, for example JAK#2. Offensive names are blocked or removed. Your four-digit code proves a score is yours and signs you in on another device: keep it private. Everyone is on the Everyone board. Students and teachers of Presentation Brothers College can also join the Students or Teachers board with their school email address, which we may check.

        5. Prizes

        Prizes are awarded once, when the game closes (the date is on the Prizes page): for the highest score on the Everyone, Students and Teachers boards, the best single light, and the best score on a player's first play. The prizes are described on the Prizes page and have no cash alternative. No prize counts spending; more plays give more chances at a best score, which is why spending is capped. Equal scores on a board: the earlier one wins. Equal best lights are split by each player's next-best light, then the one after; equal first plays by that play's best light, then the next. Only a person's first player counts for the First play prize. Winners are told by email, or in school, within 14 days of the close, collect by showing their initials and code, and receive their prizes within six weeks. Winners' initials and scores stay on the boards.

        5. Honours

        When the game closes, the top of the Everyone, Students and Teachers boards, the best single light and the best first play are honoured, as described on the Honours page. There are no prizes of money or money's worth. Equal scores are split as on the boards: the earlier one wins, and equal best lights go to the better next-best light.

        6. Fair play

        Each light scores up to 999 by how close to the mark you stop it, timed to the millisecond from the moment your finger touches the screen, which keeps differences between phones small. Everyone plays the same three lights on a given day, and nothing in a score is random. Our server checks every score. We may cancel scores or remove a player for cheating or tampering, and we will say why. If you disagree with a decision, email us: we will look again and reply.

        7. Refunds and problems

        Unused plays can be refunded if you ask within 14 days of buying them, or if the game ends before you can use them. A play can't be refunded once it has been used. If a play fails because of a fault on our side (it freezes, or the score is not saved), we will give you a new play or a refund. Each player can spend at most €20 a day and €100 in all; the You tab shows what you have donated. Payments are processed by Stripe, and we never see your card number. Your rights under Irish consumer law are not affected.

        8. Changes

        We only change these terms for a good reason, such as fixing a fault, stopping cheating or following the law, and we post the change here first. We won't raise the price of plays you have bought or take away a prize we have announced. If we end the game early, we refund unused plays.

        9. Questions and complaints

        Tell us first and we will reply within 7 days. You can also raise a concern about how a charity raises money with the Charities Regulator. These terms are governed by the laws of Ireland.

        10. About the facts

        Facts about PresLink on this site come from the school's Pres Gazette newsletters and presentationbrothers.org.

        Privacy

        Last updated 28 October 2026

        In short. We keep as little as we can, only to run the game and tell winners, never for marketing, and we delete it when the game is over. No cookies, no tracking, no ads.

        Who is responsible

        PresLink, the student charity committee of Presentation Brothers College, The Mardyke, Cork T12 VK49, with staff mentors, is responsible for your data. You can contact us through the school.

        What we keep, and why

        Your initials, code, board, scores, taps and play times: to run the game and the boards, and to check that scores are fair (our legitimate interest in running a fair game).

        The kind of device you play on (for example “iOS Safari”): to check that no kind of phone has an advantage (the same interest). Nothing that identifies your phone is kept.

        Payments (amount, time, Stripe's reference, the email you give Stripe for your receipt, and which poster or post the visit came from): to take payment and keep accounts (our contract with you, and the law on charity accounts).

        Your email, if you give it (it is optional): only to tell you if you win (your consent). If you are under 16, give a parent's or guardian's email instead. Your school email, if you give it: to put you on the Students or Teachers board (your consent).

        Who sees it

        The committee and its staff mentors (emails, only to contact winners). Our service providers handle it for us under data processing agreements: Supabase (our database, in London), Netlify (the website) and Stripe (payments). Where data leaves the EU (to the UK or the US), it is protected by an EU adequacy decision or the EU's standard contractual clauses. We never sell it or use it for marketing.

        How long we keep it

        Game data and emails are deleted within 30 days of the end of the game. Payment records are kept for as long as the school's accounting rules require.

        On your device

        Your browser's own storage remembers your player and your settings, so the game works when you come back. We use no cookies, analytics or ads. Clearing your browser's data signs you out: sign back in with your initials and code.

        Your rights

        You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, withdraw your consent, or object, by emailing us with your initials and code. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.
