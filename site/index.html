        Terms & conditions

        Last updated 22 October 2026

        1. Who runs this

        PresLink Arcade is run by the PresLink student committee of Presentation Brothers College, Cork (“the Committee”), with staff mentors. Questions: [committee contact email].

        2. What it is

        A game of skill. Each play costs €2, or €5 for 3 plays and €10 for 7. All payments, less Stripe's card-processing fees, go to PresLink for the Presentation Brothers' schools and projects in Ghana and Nigeria.

        3. Who can play

        Students and staff of Presentation Brothers College and their supporters. Under 18s must have a parent's or guardian's permission before paying. The Committee may refuse or remove any player.

        4. Your name and claim code

        You play under two or three letters of your choice. If someone already has them you are shown with a number, for example JAK#2. Offensive names are blocked or removed. Your four-digit claim code proves a score is yours and signs you in on another phone. Keep it private.

        5. How winning works

        A competition week runs Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59, Irish time. The highest score of the week on the Students board and on the Teachers board wins. Equal scores: the earlier one wins. Winners are announced by the Committee [where and when] and must show their name and claim code to collect. The prize is set by the Committee each week and has no cash alternative.

        6. Fair play

        One tap per play. Scores are checked by our server. Plays may be voided and players removed for cheating or tampering. The Committee's decision is final.

        7. Payments and refunds

        Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see your card number. Plays are not refundable except where the law requires it or a fault on our side meant a paid play could not be used. Contact the Committee and we will put it right.

        8. Your data

        We store your letters, whether you play on the student or teacher board, your scores and play times, and a record of each payment (amount, time, Stripe reference and the email you give Stripe for your receipt). We do not ask for your name. Data is held by Supabase (UK/EU) and Stripe and is deleted when the campaign ends. To have it removed sooner, contact the Committee with your letters and claim code.

        9. Availability

        The game may be unavailable at times. We are not responsible for plays lost because of your device, network or browser, though we will try to help.

        10. Changes and law

        The Committee may change these terms, prices or prizes, or end the game, at any time. These terms are governed by the laws of Ireland.
