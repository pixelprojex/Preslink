Words to use

For students

Can you stop the light? Three lights, one tap each, and the closest scores top the leaderboard. Practice is free; plays are €2 (3 for €5, 7 for €10), and every euro after card fees goes to PresLink for the Presentation Brothers' schools in Ghana and Nigeria. Under 18s need a parent's or guardian's OK to pay.

For parents and staff

PresLink Arcade is open: a quick game of skill where every €2 play funds the Presentation Brothers' schools in Ghana and Nigeria. Practice is free, and spending is capped at €20 a day.

Posters

A3 posterCorridors, the canteen, shop windows A4 posterNoticeboards, doors, handouts
  • Print at actual size (100%), not “fit to page”.
  • Hang them where people wait: the canteen queue, reception, the sports hall. Keep the code at about chest height.
  • Each poster's code is tagged (A3 or A4), so the payments show which posters work.
